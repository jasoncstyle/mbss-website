# Homepage Strategy & Wireframe

## Single Job

Help visitors **find the right course and book it.**

Everything else supports that goal.

---

## First Principle

The homepage is **not** there to teach sailing. It answers four questions:

1. Can you teach me to sail?
2. What course should I take?
3. Why should I learn from you?
4. How do I get started?

---

## What the Current Site Lacks

- Clear hierarchy and pathway
- Strong differentiation
- Strong course selection guidance

Beginners often see multiple ASA numbers before understanding where to start.

**The new homepage is a guide — not a brochure, catalog, or encyclopedia.**

---

## Mandatory Feature: Interactive Course Finder

Placed **directly below the Hero.**

| Question | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Have you sailed before? | Experience level |
| What is your goal? | Intent |
| Do you want a liveaboard experience? | Package fit |

**Optional additions:** Interested in cruising? Interested in catamarans?

**Output:** Recommended course/package + Book Now button.

Highest-converting element potential — eliminates "where do I start?"

## Section Wireframe (12 Sections)

### Section 1 — Hero

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Height | 80–100% viewport |
| Background | Full-width video loop (15–30 sec, muted, no audio) |
| Video content | Student steering → instructor coaching → docking → sail trim → students smiling → ocean sailing |
| **Avoid** | Drone shots, boats in slips |
| Headline | ASA Sailing Lessons & Certifications |
| Subheadline | Learn to sail through a proven certification pathway while gaining real-world coastal and ocean sailing experience. |
| Primary CTA | Find Your Course |
| Secondary CTA | View Upcoming Courses |
| Trust bar | ASA Certified School · Beginner Friendly · Small Class Sizes · Myrtle Beach, SC · Real Coastal & Ocean Sailing Experience |

**Conversion goal:** "I can learn here."

### Section 2 — Choose Your Path

Four large **image-based** cards (not icons):

| Card | Courses | Copy | Button |
|------|---------|------|--------|
| Learn to Sail | ASA 101 & 103 | Start your sailing journey. | Explore Courses |
| Advance Your Skills | ASA 102, 104, 106 & 114 | Build cruising confidence. | View Courses |

| Online Certifications | ASA 105, 107, 117, 119, 120 | Flexible online learning. | View Online Courses |

**Conversion goal:** Reduce confusion.

### Section 3 — Your Sailing Pathway

```
START HERE → ASA 101 → ASA 103 → Choose Your Direction
  → ASA 102 / ASA 104 → Advanced Training → ASA 106 / ASA 114
  → Online Endorsements (105, 107, 117, 119, 120)
```

CTA: **View Full Certification Path**

**Conversion goal:** "What comes next?"

### Section 4 — Featured Courses

| Course | Label | Pricing |
|--------|-------|---------|
| ASA 101 | Learn to Sail | Starting at $XXX |
| ASA 103 | Basic Coastal Cruising | Starting at $XXX |

## Mobile Strategy

Expect **65–80% mobile traffic.** Reorder sections:

1. Hero
2. Choose Your Path
3. **Upcoming Courses** (moved up — stronger purchase intent on phones)
4. Featured Courses
5. Reviews
6. Liveaboard Packages
7. Remaining sections

---

## Homepage SEO Lead

Lead with **ASA Sailing Lessons & Certifications** for SEO, revenue, and business sale value. Introduce real-world experience as differentiator in subheadline and Why MBSS section.
