# Liveaboard Package Page Strategy

## Core Positioning

**Do not sell course bundles. Sell a complete sailing transformation experience.**

| Don't Say | Do Say |
|-----------|--------|
| Combo Courses | Liveaboard Certification Packages |
| Course Bundles | Immersive Sailing Certification Packages |

"Combo" feels transactional. "Package" feels valuable. "Immersive" feels transformational.

---

## Business Purpose

Answer:
1. Why should I spend more on this package?
2. Why this package instead of individual courses?

**Revenue profile:** Highest average order value · highest revenue per student · highest profit per lead.

Drivers: more certifications, more days aboard, more immersion, private upgrade opportunities.

## Universal Page Structure (14 Sections)

All package pages use this identical layout.

### Section 1 — Hero

- Immersive image: students **actively sailing/learning** — not sitting, posing, or drinking at anchor
- Headline example: ASA 101 + 103 + 104 Liveaboard Certification Package
- Subheadline: Build skills through immersive liveaboard experience while earning multiple certifications and real-world cruising experience.
- Quick facts: Duration · Certifications Earned · Starting Price · Deposit · Private Upgrade Available
- CTA: **Book This Package** / Reserve Your Spot

### Section 2 — Who This Package Is For

**Perfect For:**
- Future cruisers, boat owners, couples sailing together, future charterers, retirees preparing for cruising, students seeking accelerated learning

**Not Ideal For:**
- Students seeking only one certification
- Those unable to commit to multi-day training

### Section 3 — Why Choose a Liveaboard Package?

Emotional, icon-driven (not long paragraphs):
- More Time On The Water
- Learn Faster
- Less Repetition
- Greater Confidence
- Better Value
- Real Cruising Experience

### Section 4 — What You'll Learn

Structured by certification with key outcomes per ASA level included in package.

### Section 5 — Experience Timeline

Day-by-day itinerary transforms "course" into "experience."

Example structure:
- Day 1: Orientation, safety, basic sailing
- Day 2: Boat handling, sail trim, docking
- Day 3: Coastal navigation, cruising skills
- Day 4: Anchoring, provisioning, systems
- Day 5: Bareboat operations, cruising scenarios
- Day 6: Assessment, certification

Customize per package duration.

### Section 6 — Life Aboard

Sell the experience. Photos: cabins, galley, cockpit, nav station, students underway.

Topics: sleeping aboard, cooking, watchstanding, anchoring, daily routine.

### Section 7 — Private Upgrade

**Visually distinct section** — not a buried checkbox.

> Traveling as a Couple, Pair of Friends, or Family? Upgrade to private instruction for an additional $500.

Benefits: private instruction, flexible pacing, personalized coaching, exclusive boat access.

CTA: **Make This Course Private**

### Section 8 — Why MBSS

Differentiated from standard course pages:
- Small Class Sizes
- Ocean Sailing Exposure
- Experienced Instructors
- Real Cruising Environment
- Structured Certification Pathway

Visitor is choosing between **schools**, not certifications.

### Section 9 — Student Success Stories

Package-specific reviews only. Format: certifications completed, photo, location, story. Video testimonials preferred.

### Section 10 — Package Comparison

Self-selection table:

| Package | Certifications | Duration | Best For |
|---------|----------------|----------|----------|
| 101 + 103 | Beginner | X days | First-time students |
| 101 + 103 + 104 | Future cruisers | X days | Fast progression |
| 103 + 104 | Existing sailors | X days | Charter readiness |
| 102 + 104 | Skill expansion | X days | Intermediate sailors |
| 104 + 114 | Catamaran focus | X days | Future charterers |

### Section 11 — Pricing

Fully transparent:

```
Package Price:     $2,495
Deposit Today:     $500
Remaining Balance: $1,995
Private Upgrade:   +$500
```

### Section 12 — Available Dates

Live availability: dates, seats remaining, instructor, Book Now.

Show scarcity (1–3 seats left).

### Section 13 — FAQ (Package-Specific)

- Previous experience required?
- What to bring?
- Sleep aboard?
- Meals included?
- Couples together?
- Private upgrade available?
- Certifications earned?

### Section 14 — Final CTA

Large emotional image.

| Element | Copy |
|---------|------|
| Headline | Accelerate Your Sailing Journey |
| Supporting | Earn multiple certifications while gaining the confidence and experience that only immersive liveaboard training can provide. |
| CTA | Reserve Your Spot |
