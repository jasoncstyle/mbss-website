# Brand Identity & Visual Design System

## Strategic Design Goal

When someone lands on MBSS they should think:

> "This feels more professional than I expected."

**Not:** "This looks like another sailing school."

Position as a **premium educational brand that happens to teach sailing** — not a sailing club, charter company, or adventure camp.

---

## What to Avoid

- Pirate themes, nautical clip art, rope borders, wave dividers
- Cartoon sailboats, stock sailing photos, overly tropical branding
- "Sailing camp" vibes
- Blue-on-blue-on-blue with boat photos and flags

---

## Design Inspiration

| Category | Borrow |
|----------|--------|
| Premium Education | Clean hierarchy, structured pathways, clear nav, strong CTAs |
| Premium Travel | Large imagery, destination-quality photography, emotional appeal |
| Premium Outdoor Brands | Confidence, authenticity, storytelling |

**Target in one sentence:** Apple meets Patagonia meets a premium sailing school.

---

## Layout Style

- Large whitespace
- Large typography
- Full-width photography
- Clean card layouts
- Minimal visual clutter
- Subtle background color changes between sections (not fancy animations)

---

## Typography

| Role | Font | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Headings** | Manrope or Plus Jakarta Sans | Modern, clean, premium, readable, not trendy |
| **Body** | Inter | Standard for premium brands today |

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Primary** | Deep Ocean Navy | Sparingly — not everywhere |
| **Secondary** | Warm Sand | Coastline-inspired |
| **Accent** | Sea Glass Green | Buttons, highlights, CTAs only |
| **Neutrals** | Off-white backgrounds | Not pure white — premium feel |

---

## Design System Components

### Cards
- Rounded corners
- Subtle shadows
- Minimal borders

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled
- **Secondary:** Outlined

### Icons
- Simple line icons
- **Not** nautical icons

### Experience Cards
Large cards for: Learn to Sail, Advance Your Skills, Liveaboard Packages, Online Certifications.

### Card-Based Scheduling
Upcoming courses as modern cards — not spreadsheet tables.

### Visual Learning Paths
ASA progression as a reusable graphic element across the site.

---

## Photography Strategy

### Show (Outcomes / Transformation)
- Students steering, docking, navigating, anchoring, learning
- Couples sailing together, planning routes, celebrating
- Instructors teaching, coaching, demonstrating

### Avoid
- Random boat photos, boats at docks, empty boats, sunset glamour shots

**You're selling transformation, not boats.**

### Hero Image (Site-Wide Standard)
Student at the helm, instructor nearby, ocean visible, action happening → communicates "Learn Here."

---

## Video Strategy

| Type | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Homepage hero** | 15–30 sec, silent loop: steering → coaching → docking → sail trim → students smiling |
| **Course videos** | 1–2 minutes |
| **Instructor videos** | Short introductions |
| **Student testimonials** | Highest trust value |

---

## Section-Specific Design

### Reviews
- Large format, one at a time (no tiny slider)
- Large photo + review + name + course (e.g., "ASA 101 & 103 Student")
- Video reviews featured first

### Instructors
- Large professional portraits — not tiny circular headshots
- Experience, certifications, teaching philosophy visible

### Private Upgrade
- Visually distinct section — not a buried checkbox
- Treat as a product with benefits and testimonials

---

## Visual Hierarchy Rule

**One primary CTA per page.** Everything else is secondary.

---

## Accessibility

**WCAG AA minimum** — contractual deliverable.

---

## Brand Positioning Summary

MBSS should feel like:

> A premium educational institution that teaches sailing.

This positioning:
- Converts beginners better
- Supports ASA revenue
- Increases trust and business value
- Makes future ownership easier
