# Information Architecture & Sitemap

## Strategic Goal

Create the simplest possible structure for visitors while building the strongest SEO foundation and highest conversion potential.

**Organize around learning paths, not course numbers.** ASA numbers remain visible, but navigation always answers: *"What should I do next?"*

---

## Primary Navigation

| Nav Item | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Home** | First impression, pathway guidance, bookings |
| **Learn to Sail** | Convert beginners (most important page after home) |
| **Advance Your Skills** | Serve existing sailors |
| **Liveaboard Packages** | High AOV, immersion, differentiation |
| **Online Certifications** | Scalable revenue, no scheduling/boat requirements |
| **About** | Trust, instructors, fleet, safety philosophy |
| **Resources** | SEO engine (blog, FAQ, guides) |
| **Reviews** | Social proof (not just testimonials) |
| **Book Now** | Guided "Find the Right Course" flow |

## Book Now Strategy

**Not a simple calendar.** Becomes a guided enrollment experience:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Choose path: Learn to Sail / Advance / Liveaboard / Online |
| 2 | Choose course or package |
| 3 | Choose date |
| 4 | Choose upgrade options (e.g., private upgrade) |
| 5 | Register / Reserve Your Spot |

Reduces overwhelm. Mirrors booking system architecture in [07-booking-system.md](07-booking-system.md).

## Ocean Sailing Adventures

**Do not** heavily promote on homepage or primary navigation.

Cross-link strategically for advanced students (e.g., post-ASA 104 lifecycle email). MBSS priority: ASA revenue and business value.

---

## Internal Linking Rule

Every content page must link to at least one of:
- A course page
- A liveaboard package
- The booking flow

Nothing exists in isolation.
