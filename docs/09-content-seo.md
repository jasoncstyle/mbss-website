# Content, SEO & AI Search Strategy

## Core Philosophy

Capture students **earlier in the decision process** — not only when they already know what they're looking for.

**Treat content as a Student Acquisition System** — a business asset that generates leads regardless of ownership.

---

## Three Visitor Types

| Type | Intent | Example Searches | Served By |
|------|--------|------------------|-----------|
| **Ready to Buy** | High intent | ASA sailing lessons, sailing school near me, ASA 101 Myrtle Beach | Course pages, booking |
| **Researching** | Pre-purchase | How long to learn to sail? What ASA course? How much do lessons cost? | Content pillars |
| **Dreamers** | 6–24 month horizon | How to become a cruiser? Charter a sailboat? Learn to sail after retirement | Cruising lifestyle content |

---

## Four Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Learn to Sail
Capture beginners. Feeds ASA 101, ASA 103.

Examples:
- How Long Does It Take to Learn to Sail?
- Can I Learn to Sail With No Experience?
- What Does ASA 101 Teach?
- What Course After ASA 101?
- How Much Do Sailing Lessons Cost?
- Is Sailing Hard To Learn?

### Pillar 2 — Sailing Certifications
Capture ASA-related searches.

Examples:
- ASA 101 vs ASA 103
- ASA 104 Explained
- Is ASA Certification Worth It?
- What Is ASA 114?
- ASA 106 Advanced Coastal Cruising Guide
- Complete ASA Certification Pathway

### Pillar 3 — Cruising Lifestyle
MBSS advantage — most schools ignore this.

Examples:
- How To Prepare For Cruising Retirement
- Buying Your First Sailboat
- Chartering In The Caribbean
- Cruising The Bahamas
- Bareboat Charter Requirements
- How Much Does Cruising Cost?

### Pillar 4 — Real-World Sailing Experience
Differentiator content.

Examples:
- What Is Coastal Cruising?
- Ocean Sailing For Beginners
- What Is Passage Training?
- Sailing Overnight Offshore
- Understanding Watch Schedules
- First Time Anchoring Overnight

## Three-Layer SEO Strategy

### Layer 1 — Money Pages (Highest Conversion)
- ASA 101, 103, 104
- Liveaboard packages
- Booking flow

### Layer 2 — Supporting Content
- What Does ASA 103 Teach?
- ASA 101 vs ASA 103
- ASA 104 Requirements

Links to money pages.

### Layer 3 — Authority Content
- Cruising guides, navigation, weather, chartering
- Builds trust and rankings

## AI Search Strategy

Optimize for: Google, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews.

### Every Content Page Structure

1. **Clear Question** (H1) — e.g., What Is ASA 104?
2. **Direct Answer** — one concise paragraph
3. **Detailed Explanation** — supporting content
4. **FAQ Section** — critical for AI and SEO
5. **Related Courses** — conversion CTA

---

| Schema | Use |
