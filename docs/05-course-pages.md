# Course Page Ecosystem

## Strategic Context

Most Google traffic will land on **course pages** — especially ASA 101, 103, and 104 — not the homepage or package pages.

Course pages should feel like **product pages**, not educational curriculum pages. You're selling transformation; certification is proof.

---

## Five Questions Every Course Page Must Answer

1. Is this the right course for me?
2. What will I learn?
3. Why should I take it at MBSS?
4. What should I take next?
5. How do I enroll?

---

## Universal Framework (11 Sections)

Every on-water course page uses this **identical structure**.

### Section 1 — Hero

- Large image: **student learning/doing/succeeding** — not boat, sail, or marina
- Headline: outcome-first (see [02-brand-messaging.md](02-brand-messaging.md))
- Subheadline: confidence and skills framing
- Quick facts bar: Duration · Price · Deposit · Location · Experience Required
- Primary CTA: Book [Course] / Reserve Your Spot
- Secondary CTA: View Dates

### Section 2 — Is This Course Right For You?

**First content below hero** — not course description or ASA standards.

- **Perfect For:** audience-specific list
- **No Experience Required** (where applicable) — very visible

Addresses: *"Am I ready for this?"*

### Section 3 — What You'll Learn

Scannable **cards** — not giant bullet lists.

Examples: Boat Handling · Sail Trim · Points of Sail · Docking Basics · Safety · Crew Communication

### Section 4 — What You'll Be Able To Do

Post-completion outcomes (critical — most schools miss this).

Example (ASA 101):
- Safely sail in moderate conditions
- Leave and return to a dock
- Trim sails effectively
- Crew confidently aboard a sailboat
- Continue toward coastal cruising certifications

### Section 5 — Why Learn at MBSS?

Five cards — **Why MBSS, not Why ASA:**
- Small Class Sizes
- Experienced Instructors
- Coastal Sailing Environment
- Ocean Sailing Exposure
- Proven Certification Pathway

### Section 6 — Course Pathway

Visual: "You Are Here" → current course → next recommended step.

| Completed | Recommend Next |
|-----------|----------------|
| ASA 101 | ASA 103 |
| ASA 103 | ASA 104 + 101+103+104 Liveaboard Package |
| ASA 104 | ASA 114 and/or ASA 106 |

CTA: **Continue Your Sailing Journey**

### Section 7 — Student Reviews

Course-specific only. Format: graduate label, photo, review, location. Video preferred.

### Section 8 — Upcoming Dates

On-page — never force visitors to leave.

```
June 15–17 | 3 Seats Left | $250 Deposit | Book Now
```

Show scarcity: 1 Seat Left · 2 Seats Left · 3 Seats Left

### Section 9 — Pricing

Transparent — no "Call for Pricing."

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Course Price | $695 |
| Deposit Today | $250 |
| Remaining Balance | $445 |
| Note | Final balance invoiced separately (Square today → Stripe future) |
| Private Options | When applicable |

### Section 10 — FAQ

Unique per course. Supports SEO, AI search, and conversion.

**ASA 101 examples:** experience required? what to wear? age requirement? what after 101? couples?

### Section 11 — Final CTA

Large image, student at helm.

Headline: Start Your Sailing Journey (adapt per course)

CTA: Book [Course] / Reserve Your Spot
